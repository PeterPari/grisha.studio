Fire on the Hill

Date May 7–23, 2026
Venue Ruth and Elmer Wellin Museum of Art, Hamilton College, Clinton, NY
About

Fire on the Hill was a group exhibition at the Ruth and Elmer Wellin Museum of Art, Hamilton College, on view May 7–23, 2026. Gregory Parizhsky presented three sculptures, Tower of Babel, The Wishing Well, and Wood Gong, alongside work by fellow Hamilton College artists.
