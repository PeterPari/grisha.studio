Hidden Layers

Physical model  ·  Object study, 1/6" scale
Scroll
Statement Gregory Parizhsky
2024

The concept I extrapolated from the Shiny Tea Store was that a space becomes unique and human when there are multiple viewing angles. Deconstructing the store to its bare bones, I created a model that attempted to embody this idea through form and layering.
