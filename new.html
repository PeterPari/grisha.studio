The Artist

Gregory Parizhsky is an NYC based sculptor who utilizes a range of materials to create large scale abstract works. Central concepts to his practice include philosophy and literature, and recently, an exploration of religion and how different cultures interact with shrines.

Currently studying Studio Art and Economics at Hamilton College, his work seeks to bridge the gap between abstract theoretical concepts and tangible, spatial realities.
