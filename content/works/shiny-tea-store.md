Searching for a meaningful building among the myriad of structures in New York City, I landed on a small bubble tea store that I once frequented and then worked at. The drawings show the narrow yet deceptively long storefront. The structure emphasizes that space does not need to be immense to build community. The store grounds the egos of the architects that erected the buildings to either side.
